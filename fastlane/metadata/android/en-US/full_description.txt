DP Call is a minimalist dialer for peer-to-peer voice calls. There are no accounts, no phone numbers and no developer-run servers: the conversation goes directly between the two phones (WebRTC, DTLS-SRTP encryption).

<b>How it works</b>

Every user gets a code, which is their public key. Exchange codes, add each other to contacts and call: the other phone rings even when the app is closed.

The call request travels end-to-end encrypted through public Nostr relays. A relay cannot see who is calling whom, the codes or the connection details — only an IP address and a timestamp. If the other side is offline, the invitation can be sent manually through any messenger.

<b>Features</b>

* Incoming calls like a regular dialer: Accept / Decline notification, shown over the lock screen
* Calls continue when the app is minimized and can be ended from the notification
* Answer and hang up with wired and Bluetooth headset buttons
* Mute, audio output selection: earpiece, speaker, headset, Bluetooth
* Noise suppression in both directions, echo cancellation, Opus at 48 kHz
* Connection quality indicator
* Calls survive a network switch (Wi-Fi ↔ mobile)
* QR codes for exchanging contacts, call history
* Calls from codes outside your contacts ring as “Unknown caller”; a “Contacts only” mode is available

<b>Good to know</b>

* Voice only: no video, chats or files
* To receive calls in the background the app keeps a persistent connection to the relays (a silent notification) — no Google Play Services or push services involved
* Public STUN servers are used for NAT traversal; when a direct connection is impossible a TURN relay is needed — you can set your own in Settings
* The key never leaves the device; reinstalling the app changes your code
* Interface languages: English and Russian
